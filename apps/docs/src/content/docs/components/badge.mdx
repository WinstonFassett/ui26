## Under the hood

- **Shared config:** `apps/docs/registry/Badge/Badge.tv.ts` — colocated `tv()` call.
- **Wrappers:** `apps/docs/registry/Badge/Badge.{tsx,svelte,astro}` — thin passthroughs rendering `<span>` with `cn(badge({variant}), className)`.
- **Error state:** driven by `aria-invalid` rather than a `destructive` *ambient* variant — pass `aria-invalid` when surfacing a validation error.
