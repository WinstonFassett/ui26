Same `Switch` rendered three times per row — React, Svelte, Astro. Hidden `<input type="checkbox">` drives `:checked` state, which Tailwind's `has-[:checked]:` and `peer-checked:` selectors translate into track color + thumb position. No JS.

## Under the hood

- **Shared config:** `apps/docs/registry/Switch/Switch.tv.ts` — two `tv()` calls (`switchRoot` track + `switchThumb`). No variants.
- **Wrappers:** `apps/docs/registry/Switch/Switch.{tsx,svelte,astro}` — a `<label>` wrapping a visually-hidden native `<input type="checkbox">` and a styled thumb `<span>`.
- **State:** driven entirely by `:checked` on the input. `has-[:checked]:` on the root flips track color, `peer-checked:` on the thumb flips translate-x.
- **Error state:** pass `aria-invalid` on the Switch and `has-[[aria-invalid=true]]:` on the root surfaces the destructive ring.
